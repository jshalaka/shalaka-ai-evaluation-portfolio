COMPLETED DESIGN

AI Quality Assurance Framework

A lifecycle model for governing AI quality from specification to production evidence. The framework translates responsible-AI principles into engineering controls.

01Golden datasetsExpert-labelled, versioned, cohort-aware
02Evaluator stackDeterministic + model-based + human
03Calibrated gatesSample size, confidence and pinned judges
04Production signalsSampling, drift and trace monitoring
05Failure flywheelIncidents become permanent regressions
Designed for regulated delivery

Human approval remains explicit at specification, merge and release. The evidence pack connects datasets, evaluator versions, thresholds, exceptions and outcomes for engineering, risk and audit review.
